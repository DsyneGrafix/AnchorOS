## Capability Gained

AnchorOS gained a single authoritative platform identity and a professional startup experience.

Before BOOT-0011, the platform began with a simple startup message. After BOOT-0011, AnchorOS identified itself by product, version, codename, stage, Boot number, build number, and company before initialization began.

## Objective

Separate three responsibilities:

- `version.py` owns platform identity.
- `banner.py` owns startup presentation.
- `startup.py` owns platform initialization.

## Architectural Change

```text
version.py
    ↓
banner.py
    ↓
startup.py
    ↓
AnchorOS initialization
```

This removed duplicated release metadata and established one source of truth for future interfaces such as the CLI, Admin Console, REST API, diagnostics, documentation, and installers.

## Why It Matters

BOOT-0011 changed the first impression of AnchorOS. The platform no longer appeared to be a Python script that happened to run modules. It presented itself as a versioned operating platform with a stable identity.

## Architectural Principles Reinforced

> One source of truth for platform identity.

> Presentation shall not own platform state.

> Startup orchestration shall not duplicate release metadata.

## Historical Significance

BOOT-0011 was the point at which AnchorOS became recognizable as a product, not merely an architecture.
