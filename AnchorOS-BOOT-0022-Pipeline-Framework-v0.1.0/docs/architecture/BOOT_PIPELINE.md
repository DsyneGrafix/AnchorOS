AnchorOS Boot Pipeline

Stage 1
Banner

Purpose:
Identify the running platform.

Output:
Startup Banner

-----------------------------------------

Stage 2
Discovery

Purpose:
Locate all AnchorCore services and Frameworks.

Output:
Registered Modules

-----------------------------------------

Stage 3
Registration

Purpose:
Populate the Service Registry.

-----------------------------------------

Stage 4
Startup

Purpose:
Initialize all services.

-----------------------------------------

Stage 5
Verification

Purpose:
Confirm startup integrity.

-----------------------------------------

Stage 6
Reporting

Purpose:
Summarize operational state.

-----------------------------------------

Stage 7
Operational

Purpose:
Transition the platform to operational status.
