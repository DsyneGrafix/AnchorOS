## Purpose

Apply bounded local rules to determine what a persisted opportunity and its
active evidence currently support about Florida geography, infrastructure
classification, organization, service territory, environmental exposure,
regulatory dependencies, ownership, unknowns, source dates, confidence, and
evidence cautions.

## Behavior

The module consumes only non-archived evidence. Archived evidence IDs are
recorded as excluded, never silently consumed. Text fields may support a
`Partially Supported` finding but do not become independently verified facts.
Absent support is recorded as an unknown, assumption, risk, or missing-evidence
item.

The reference `EV-000001` produces Moderate confidence because it is bounded
Sirius Logic Systems reference analysis with material gaps. It is not an
official Florida Power & Light record and does not establish endorsement,
procurement intent, ownership, service territory, funding availability, or
regulatory approval.

## Limitations

This module does not browse current sources, determine legal rights, validate
engineering designs, establish environmental or regulatory compliance, verify
ownership, prove customer intent, determine funding, or make an investment or
commercial recommendation. Dated independent evidence is required before
high-confidence conclusions.
